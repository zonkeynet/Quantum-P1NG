# The voice of qp1ng.com

How the English pages are written. Translations follow the same voice: a slogan is rewritten so it hits in the
target language too, not translated word for word.

## Who reads it

Journalists, activists and people under censorship; hackers, radio operators and privacy people. They know the
subject, they are in a hurry, and they have read every "military-grade" promise before.

## Tone

- Hacker ethic and freedom of information: information wants to move, people decide who sees it.
- Confident, short, technical. Lead with what the app does, then how, with a real name or number
  (AES-256-GCM, X25519MLKEM768, Tor 0.4.9, 433 MHz, Whisper), never with adjectives.
- Second person and imperatives: "Pick the route.", "Strip the metadata.", not "Users can choose...".
- One idea per sentence. A body text has 1-2 sentences.
- Wit over hype. A slogan twists something the reader knows ("The cloud is someone else's computer"),
  it does not shout.
- The terminal look stays: labels in CAPS with `//` and `::` are fine, but they must be true too.

## Never

- Absolute promises: untraceable, anonymous, unhackable, unbreakable, zero traces, no metadata, eliminate,
  guaranteed, impossible, 100%, military-grade, bank-grade.
- Filler: seamless, cutting-edge, next-gen, powerful, robust, revolutionary, ultimate, leverage, empower.
- Corporate hedging: "supported", "compatible", "where possible", "helps you", "allows users to". Say the limit
  once, plainly, instead ("Needs a downloaded model.").
- Worn-out slogans: "not your keys, not your coins", "trust no one", "privacy is a right" on its own.
- Features the published app does not have, and Pro features shown as free. Pro is named where a section
  presents a Pro feature ("Pro").

## Limits are part of the voice

State a real limit in the same tone, once: "No app beats spyware on your phone.", "Public posts are public.",
"A wipe deletes keys and files; flash memory keeps no promises.", "Transmitting needs a licence where the law
says so." A limit makes the rest believable.

## Length

- Section lead: at most 35 words. Slogan (the subtitle under the title): at most 8 words.
- Card text: at most 22 words. Cards in the same row or grid stay within 3 words of each other, so the boxes line up.
- Headings: at most 5 words. Buttons: at most 3 words.
- Rewrite down by a third where the text is longer; a short text that already works stays.

## Markup

Change text only: keep tags, classes, ids, `aria-*`, `alt` meaning and entities (`&amp;`, `&rarr;`). Do not add
elements. A redundant sentence or list item may go. Labels such as "DEMO", "SAMPLE DATA" and "INTERACTIVE DEMO"
stay where they mark something that is not the real app.
