Privacy Policy

ZERO-TELEMETRY GUARANTEE • LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

® INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY NOTICE: Q-P1NG® and Quantum P1NG® are registered trademarks of ZonkeyNet to protect intellectual property.

Quantum P1NG (Q-P1NG®) is engineered from the ground up on the principle of Zero-Telemetry and Absolute Data Minimization. As a commercial security suite, our business model relies strictly on selling software licenses, never on monetizing, tracking, profiling, or aggregating user data.

1. Zero Data Collection

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not require, collect, or store your phone number, email address, legal name, contacts, or location.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking SDKs: The commercial software binary contains zero cloud analytics, zero advertising frameworks, and zero background telemetry reporting.
  • Anonymous Mailbox Hashes: For network routing, the only identifier utilized is a transient cryptographic SHA-256 hash that cannot be reverse-engineered to identify your real identity or device.
  • Local Cryptographic Keys: All cryptographic keypairs (RSA-4096, AES-256-GCM, Kyber/Dilithium) are generated locally on your hardware. Private keys never leave your device.

2. Message Handling & Zero-Knowledge Architecture

  • End-to-End Cryptography: Every communication is encrypted on the sender's device and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient.
  • Zero-Knowledge: We have no mathematical or architectural capability to decrypt, inspect, or reconstruct your messages, voice notes, or file attachments.
  • Ephemeral Dead Drops: Server nodes operate strictly as temporary transit relays. Transmitted packets are erased immediately upon delivery.

3. Anonymity & Network Connectivity

  • Zero Server Logs: Gateway nodes operate in memory and do not log client IP addresses, timestamps, or routing metadata.
  • Native Tor Integration: Q-P1NG® natively supports connections through the Tor network to obscure geographical location and physical network origins.
  • Onion Routing: Users can communicate exclusively via .onion hidden service addresses, completely bypassing standard internet gateways.

4. Offline Local AI & Media Privacy

All AI capabilities (Q-Brain text inference, Whisper speech-to-text dictation, and Stable Diffusion generation) run 100% locally on your device hardware using local weights. No audio recordings, text prompts, or generated imagery are ever transmitted to any third-party AI cloud API.
