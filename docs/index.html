BEFORE YOU GO ON AIR
Your station, explained.

      
Does Q-Cast work without the internet?
Q-Cast is internet audio streaming and needs a connection, including when using Tor. The VHF RadioBridge and LoRa tools are separate features for compatible radio hardware.

      
Does appearing on the globe reveal my GPS location?
You choose whether to publish a place and select the point yourself. Q-Cast does not automatically publish your phone's GPS position. The station details and any place you choose for a public listing are public information.

      
Can I keep my radio Tor-only?
Yes. You can broadcast and publish a station through Tor. Listing the station does not authorize a relay to expose its audio on the ordinary web; web distribution is a separate choice.

      
Can my station grow beyond a few listeners?
Use authorized repeaters or a compatible Icecast server to distribute the audio. Direct phone mode supports a small audience; larger audiences require real relay or server bandwidth. QP1NG does not promise free unlimited hosting.
