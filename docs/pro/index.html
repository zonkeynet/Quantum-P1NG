HELP

Lost your key? Questions?

Recover a card purchase

Enter the transaction number from your Paddle receipt (it starts with txn_). For Bitcoin, reopen the order link shown during payment.

Is the licence tied to my phone?

No. It is a personal licence for the devices under your control. Keep the key: you paste it again on a new phone.

Does the app contact you to check it?

No. The key is signed by us and checked on your phone against a public key inside the app. Nothing is sent to validate it.

Website or Google Play edition?

Both have the Pro features. The Play edition is bought and updated through Google Play; the website edition is unlocked with this key, updates itself from GitHub and adds the Linux shell.

How do Bitcoin payments work?

Each order gets a fresh address of our wallet and the amount is held for 24 hours. The key appears after one confirmation; a payment that arrives later still counts for two weeks.

Who processes card payments?

Paddle, our merchant of record, handles the payment, the receipt and VAT. We never see your card details.
